FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you see or store our images and data?

No, not by default. The tools we build run locally on your computer. If we need a small number of sample files during the build phase, that's a separate, explicit request, never assumed, and always limited to what's strictly necessary.

What does this cost?

It depends on the scope of what you need, which is why we don't publish fixed prices. After a free consultation, we send a written, fixed-scope quote before any work begins, so you know the cost before committing.

Do you work with omics data (transcriptomics, proteomics, etc.)?

No. We don't currently offer consulting or interpretation for omics data. Our focus is imaging, cytometry, plate assays, behaviour tracking, signal analysis and field data.

What operating systems do you support?

We aim to support whatever your lab already uses, Windows, macOS or Linux. Tell us your setup during the consultation and we'll confirm feasibility as part of the quote.

How long does a typical project take?

We deliberately keep projects small and defined, typically days to a few weeks, not months. If a request is larger than that, we'll say so upfront and discuss options, such as splitting it into phases.

What if my data doesn't work with the proposed approach?

We check feasibility using your sample files before confirming a quote. If something turns out to be technically infeasible partway through, we'll tell you directly and discuss options rather than deliver something that doesn't work.

Do we need to know how to code?

No. The tool is built so your team can run it without writing code. You will still need to follow the documented steps to use it, we'll make sure those are clear.

Who owns the resulting software?

This is defined in writing before any project starts, in our software licence terms. As a starting principle, you receive a licence to use and modify the tool for your own work.

Can you help with an existing macro or script that's not working well?

Often, yes. Bring it to the consultation and we'll assess whether it's a case of fixing and hardening what exists, or building something new.

Do you sign confidentiality agreements?

This can be discussed directly for projects that require it. Let us know during the consultation.

Do you offer training or help interpreting results?

Yes, as separate, paid add-ons, training, visualisation support and interpretation support are available beyond the base delivery, scoped individually.

Is this suitable for a student project or a small budget?

Possibly, tell us your situation on the consultation and we'll be honest about whether it's a fit. Our current focus is funded research labs, so very small budgets may need to wait or look elsewhere.

Still have a question?

Ask us directly, or go ahead and describe your analysis need.
