About

A team of experts in life science and bioinformatics

MAX Data Analysis was started to close a specific gap: researchers who need their data analysed properly, but don't have the in-house computational skills, the time, or the budget to build a solution themselves.

Scientific review

Our team includes life scientists with decades of combined research experience, who make sure each tool measures what actually matters scientifically — not just what's technically convenient to compute.

Engineering

Our team includes software engineers with bioinformatics expertise, who build each tool on established, validated open-source components rather than untested one-off code.

We're currently operating without individually named public profiles. If that matters for your decision — for instance if you need to name us in a grant or a methods section — ask us directly on a call; we're happy to discuss it.

Why we work this way

Small, defined projects on purpose

We deliberately avoid large, multi-team, long-cycle projects. That keeps turnaround short and lets us stay closely involved in every build, rather than spreading thin across engagements we can't give proper attention to.

What we don't do

We don't offer consulting or scientific interpretation of omics data (transcriptomics, proteomics, metabolomics and related fields) at this time. See our Terms of Service for the current scope.

What stays on your machine

Every tool we build runs locally. We don't require your images or data to be uploaded to us or to any third-party platform as part of normal use.
