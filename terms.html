2. Scope of services

MAX Data Analysis builds custom software for life-science data analysis. Current service scope is limited to non-omics analysis, including imaging, flow cytometry, plate-based assays, behaviour and movement tracking, electrophysiology and time-series signals, and ecological or field datasets. We do not currently offer consulting, interpretation, or analysis services for transcriptomics, proteomics, metabolomics, lipidomics, methylomics, or multi-omics data. This scope may change; any change will be reflected in an updated version of these terms.
