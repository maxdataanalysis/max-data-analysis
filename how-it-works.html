Process

A defined process, not an open-ended engagement

Every project follows the same five steps. We keep scope small and timelines short on purpose, most projects are measured in days or a few weeks, not months.

1

Free 15-minute consultation

You describe what you're trying to measure and how you do it today. If you have a few sample files, it helps, but it isn't required to have a first conversation. We'll tell you honestly, on the call, whether it looks feasible.

2

Scoping and a written, fixed-scope quote

We confirm the measurement, the data format, and what "done" looks like, then send a fixed-scope quote in writing. Nothing is built before you accept it. If a request turns out to be too large or unclear for a fixed scope, we'll say so and discuss options rather than guess.

3

Build

We assemble and configure the analysis using established, validated open-source components rather than writing image processing from scratch, it's faster, and it means the underlying methods are ones the field already trusts.

4

Delivery, with documentation

You receive an installable package for your operating system, plain-language instructions for running it, and a description of its known limitations, what it's built to handle and what's outside its scope.

5

Support window

A defined period of email support is included after delivery for questions and bug fixes. Training, visualisation help, interpretation support, and further development beyond the original scope are available separately.

What we need from you

Good input makes for a faster, more accurate tool

Helpful to have

  • A clear description of the measurement you need
  • A handful of representative sample files
  • The instrument, data type and file format
  • Any existing macro, script, or manual process you're trying to replace

What you'll receive

  • A tool installed and running on your own computer
  • Plain-language documentation
  • A written note of known limitations
  • A defined support window after delivery
Good to know

What's included, and what's a separate conversation

Included in delivery

The configured tool, installation instructions, documentation, and the support window agreed in your quote.

Available as a separate offer

Training sessions, visualisation support, interpretation support, further custom features, and ongoing maintenance beyond the support window, always scoped and agreed separately, never assumed.
