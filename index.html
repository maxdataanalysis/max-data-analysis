What we work with

Whatever your experiment produces, we build the tool that reads it

These are the kinds of data we're asked about most often, imaging, flow cytometry, plate assays, behaviour and movement, electrophysiology and time series, ecology and field records, and many more besides. If your data isn't here, it doesn't mean we can't help.

Imaging

Microscopy & image analysis

Turning fields of images into numbers you can put in a figure, consistently, across a whole batch.

  • Cell and nuclei counting
  • Colocalisation and intensity measurement
  • Morphology and size distributions
  • Wound-healing and time-lapse quantification
Cytometry

Flow cytometry workflows

Applying the same gating logic across every sample, so batches stay comparable and re-analysis isn't a manual job.

  • Reproducible, scripted gating
  • Population statistics across batches
  • Batch-to-batch consistency checks
  • Export into your own statistics workflow
Plate assays

qPCR, ELISA & plate readers

Getting from raw instrument exports to normalised, quality-checked results without a fragile spreadsheet.

  • Plate normalisation and controls
  • Standard and dose–response curve fitting
  • Automated QC flags for bad wells
  • Merging many plates into one dataset
Behaviour

Movement & behaviour tracking

Converting hours of recordings into trajectory metrics, without watching every frame yourself.

  • Path length, speed and turning metrics
  • Zone occupancy and entries
  • Event scoring from tracked positions
  • Batch processing across many recordings
Signals

Electrophysiology & time series

Detecting and counting events in long recordings using criteria you define once and apply everywhere.

  • Spike and event detection
  • Multi-channel batch processing
  • Trace quality control
  • Summary tables per recording
Field data

Ecology & field datasets

Cleaning and combining messy, real-world records into something you can actually analyse and plot.

  • Cleaning and merging inconsistent records
  • Automated validation rules
  • Summary statistics and indices
  • Repeatable figures for reports

The process

From first call to working tool

Five steps, start to finish. Most projects run in days to a few weeks, not months.

01
Day 0 · 15 minutes

Free consultation

You describe what you need to measure and show a few sample files if you have them. We tell you honestly whether it's feasible, and if it isn't, we say so on the call rather than after an invoice.

02
Within a few days

Fixed-scope quote

A written scope and a fixed price, covering exactly what the tool will do and what it won't. Nothing gets built until you've accepted it, so there's no open-ended bill forming in the background.

03
Days to a few weeks

We build it

Configured and tested against your own sample files, using proven open-source engines rather than untested one-off code. Reviewed from both the scientific and technical side before it reaches you.

04
Delivery day

Installed & documented

The tool on your own machine, with plain-language instructions, a note of its known limits, and the outputs in a format you can take straight into your own analysis.

05
After delivery

Support window

A defined period of email support for questions and fixes, agreed in your quote. Training, extra features and longer support are available separately, never assumed, never billed by surprise.

Why it costs less

Priced for a research budget, not an enterprise one

We're not cheap by cutting corners, we're cheaper because of how the work is scoped. Three specific reasons:

01

We build on proven open-source engines

We don't write analysis from scratch. We assemble and configure established, widely-used scientific libraries, so you're paying for the configuration and packaging, not for reinventing methods the field already trusts.

02

Small, fixed scope

One tool, one job, agreed in writing before work starts. No open-ended engagements, no discovery phase billed by the month, no scope creep priced back to you.

03

No platform to pay for

The tool runs on computers you already own. There's no per-seat licence, no annual subscription, and no cloud storage bill attached to your data.

Every project is quoted individually after a free consultation, so you see the number before committing to anything.
